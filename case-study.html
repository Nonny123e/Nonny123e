THE OPPORTUNITY · 02 / 05

Make a tactile category easier to navigate online.

Fabric shopping is full of visual choices. This concept turns a broad catalogue into a clearer path from first inspiration to a useful enquiry.

“

Can I find the right fabric, picture it in my space, and know what to do next?

THE CUSTOMER QUESTION THIS EXPERIENCE IS DESIGNED TO ANSWER
01

Finding the right fit

Give the catalogue helpful paths through material, colour, pattern and room.

02

Making a confident choice

Pair product details with project imagery and practical service information.

03

Knowing what comes next

Make a consultation or quote request easy to find when a shopper is ready.

✳ These are design opportunities for the concept, not findings from a completed research study.

02 / 05FROM BROWSING TO CLARITY
THE EXPERIENCE · 03 / 05

A guided journey from discovery to enquiry.

Each step answers a different question, so a shopper can move at their own pace without losing the next action.

01

Discover

Browse curated collections or filter by colour, material and room.

“Show me something like this.”
02

Compare

Read useful specifications, explore variants and see related options.

“What makes these different?”
03

Envision

Connect product inspiration to finished rooms and project stories.

“How might this look at home?”
04

Enquire

Start a quote or consultation with clear prompts and expectations.

“Help me take the next step.”
DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Keep a helpful next step visible, without interrupting the browsing experience.
