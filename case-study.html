THE CHALLENGE

Beautiful products needed a better way to be discovered.

The business needed a digital presence that could do more than display a logo and contact details. Customers needed to explore fabrics, understand curtain solutions, see completed projects and request a quote without friction.

At the same time, the owner needed a scalable content system so new products, collections, projects and articles could be added independently.

UX STRATEGY

From browsing to confidence.

01

Discover

Organise the catalogue around the way customers think: material, colour, style, room and use case.

02

Understand

Give every product enough context — specifications, applications, variants and related options.

03

Trust

Use projects, testimonials, process steps and visual evidence to reduce uncertainty.

04

Act

Make requesting a quote or consultation a natural next step throughout the experience.
